About me

Junior systems administrator in Viana do Castelo, Portugal, looking for an internship from January 2027.

I am taking the Networks and Computer Systems course at IEFP, a level 5 qualification in the Portuguese framework (EQF level 5), and I have completed the Google IT Support Professional Certificate. Alongside it I am following the Cisco Networking Academy Network Technician career path, the Linux courses for systems administration and the Microsoft Learn administrator path.

My focus is everyday infrastructure: accounts and permissions in Active Directory, Group Policy, Windows and Linux servers, switching and VLANs, and backups that can actually be restored. I build everything in my home lab first, and every project ends with a diagram, the configuration and a written procedure.

I want to join a team where I can learn from more experienced administrators, take on support and maintenance tasks from day one and earn more responsibility as I prove my work.

How I work

  1. 01

    Document while doing

    Every change is recorded with the reason, the steps and how to roll it back.

  2. 02

    A backup only counts once restored

    I test recovery regularly, not just the copy.

  3. 03

    Automate what repeats

    Anything done more than twice becomes a PowerShell or Bash script.

  4. 04

    Least privilege by default

    Separate admin accounts, and access granted only when it is needed.
